two hands, two sensors

REINS

gesture midi for guitar.

wave your hand over the body. a distance sensor in the pickguard reads how high it floats and turns that into midi. the guitar radios it to a little box on the floor. the box plays it out over usb and 5-pin din, and brings a few friends: a second sensor, an expression pedal and two footswitches.

01guitar side

the pickguard

a printed pickguard for a squier mustang. the sensor looks up through a ring of 16 leds between the pickups. everything else hides underneath: a XIAO ESP32-S3, a screen, a knob, a mute button and a lipo cell.

 

02guitar board

the transmitter

the board hangs 8.1 mm under the pickguard on four M2 screws. the XIAO plugs into two sockets and pokes up into a little hood, so its usb-c port stays on top for charging. the screen sits under a bezel in the same printed frame.

the knob turns a PEC12R encoder. the orange cap is mute. three JST PH headers go to the sensor, the lipo and the led ring. a level shifter keeps the ring's data line honest.
