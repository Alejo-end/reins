REINS

A gesture MIDI controller for a guitar.

A distance sensor in the pickguard reads how high my hand is over the body. The guitar sends that as MIDI, by radio, to a small box on the floor. The box passes it on over USB and 5-pin DIN, and adds a second sensor, an expression pedal and two footswitches.

01guitar side

The pickguard

A printed pickguard for a Squier Mustang. The sensor looks up through a ring of 16 LEDs between the pickups. The board under it carries a XIAO ESP32-S3, the screen, a knob and a mute button, and runs from a LiPo cell.

 

02guitar board

The transmitter

The board hangs 8.1 mm under the pickguard, on four M2 screws into a printed frame. The XIAO ESP32-S3 plugs into two sockets and reaches up into a hood, so its USB-C port stays on top for charging. The screen sits under a bezel in the same frame.

The knob turns a PEC12R encoder. The orange cap presses a button that mutes. Under the board, three JST PH headers go to the sensor, the LiPo cell and the LED ring. A level shifter drives the ring's data line.
